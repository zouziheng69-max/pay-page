本届 TEDx SSES Youth 以「Ripple 涟漪」为核心主题。每一个微小的选择、思考与行动，都如同投入湖面的石子，悄然扩散、持续影响自我、他人与世界。

This year’s TEDx SSES Youth centers on the theme Ripple. Every small thought, choice, and action is like a pebble falling into water. It starts subtle yet spreads endlessly, bringing lasting influence to individuals, communities, and the world.
